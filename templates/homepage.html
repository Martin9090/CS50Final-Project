Track Your Symptoms & Improve Your Health

Stay on top of your families health with this easy-to-use health tracker app. Whether you're dealing with a chronic condition or recovering from a short-term illness, this will provide you with the tools to monitor your symptoms, track medication, and stay informed about your progress.

Features

  • Track your daily symptoms
  • Record your medication and dosages
  • Monitor your temperature
  • Download information to show your doctor
  • Helpful links to the NHS

How It Works

1. Create a profile.

2. Add a patient.

3. Log your symptoms, medications and temperatures.

4. Track your progress over time.

5. Share your health data with your doctor.
